## Scope, provenance and limits

- B01–B20 retain the registered cases, tolerances and optimization routines (three DE seeds for cost-optimal converter cases). No B design exceeds 324 bar.
- A19, A21, A23, A25, A27 and A28 depend on measured Fe bed sizing; those were recomputed. Other A state/flow/thermodynamic values are independent of the Fe law. E and V are unchanged. C01–C07 and C11–C12 are recomputed from the in-range optima; C09 uses the Keestra 90-bar library.
- C08 (all historical staging groups) and C10 (multi-pressure staging correlation) are frozen campaign-level diagnostics. The available Keestra staged library covers 90 bar only. Their registered scores are explicitly carried in the 64-item comparison column; a fully Keestra-only 64-row verdict requires a new multi-pressure staging campaign. No Keestra-only claim is made for those two rows.
- The committed staged continuation library contains only FFF/FFR/FRR/RRR. RFF/RRF curves were added by local continuation at 0–12% in 0.5 percentage-point steps, initialized from all three committed global arrangement optima; their lower envelopes are evaluated at the requested 0.05 percentage-point grid. These are library-envelope estimates (upper bounds on continuously optimized costs), not fresh global optima at every rate. The four existing patterns and their window extraction are unchanged.
- Owned-metal: purchases at years 0, 10, 20; recovery at 10, 20, 25; all discounted at the metal rate. Supplier lease: no principal purchase or sale, unsecured/no deposit, annual rent and loss settlement, all discounted at the fixed 12% plant rate. Documented Pt/Pd lease rates are analogies, not published Ru quotes.
- Source `results.json` stores financing rates to six decimals. The gate asserts 1e-6 relative after rounding computed anchors to that source precision and also checks unrounded values against the rounding interval. An unrounded 1e-6 relative test of those rounded roots is not numerically supportable; raw relative errors are exposed in `canonical_anchor_checks.json`.
- The ledger includes the actual Supplementary Table 4 text in `docs/SI_TABLES.md`, linked by the SI document. Citations, figure cross-reference numbers and chemical subscripts are excluded. Numeric ranges produce endpoint rows; duplicate occurrences remain separate. CHECK means a unique semantically appropriate result key was not confirmed, including stale mixed-branch benchmark statements that need editorial separation.
