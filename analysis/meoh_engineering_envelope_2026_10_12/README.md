# Equipment-envelope follow-up to joint methanol field robustness

**Status: exploratory/unfrozen.** This branch stacks on PR #55 and does not edit the frozen S5 field outputs, previously completed joint m-p scan, manuscript, captions or figures.

## Question

Do the previously reported four same-temperature, cross-catalyst ranking reversals with >5% modelled cost regret remain material when the process *jointly reoptimizes catalyst loading and purge* while keeping the equipment scale within a transparent published-plant reference envelope?

The anchor is the single-step CO2-to-methanol design from **Campos et al., Processes 2022, 10, 1535**, which supplies the original plant cost model used in this repository. Normalizers are imported directly from the frozen-compatible \`meoh_d01_model.py\`:

- Catalyst inventory = 6 reactor modules x 478.13 t = 2,868.78 t.
- Reactor-inlet flow = \`REF_REACTOR_IN_KMOL_H\`, itself calibrated to that process with \`RECYCLE_FLOW_CORRECTION\`.
- Recycle compressor shaft power = \`REF_POWERS[2]\` MW from the anchor state and the model's assumed recycle pressure drop.
- Anchor gas recycle flow = 54,290 kmol/h; shown diagnostically.

**Do not interpret these ratios as licensed design maximums.** A single plant is not the statistical distribution of physical reactors, and scale-up by adding identical parallel modules is not explicitly reengineered.

## Full-cohort optimizations

Each candidate retains the source-reported reaction temperature, pressure, H2/CO2 feed ratio and selectivities. Conversion at loading multiplier m uses the one-point calibrated first-order surrogate of PR #55, and STY(m) is consistently rescaled. Every candidate is assessed over the purge grid 1e-6 to 40%, with both catalyst multiplier maxima (10 and 25), and with four inlet-composition scenarios (unbounded, 4.92%, 6.862515529%, 10.249% non-H2/CO2). Three physical scale indicators are recalculated **at each (m,p)**:

1. Catalyst bed inventory / 2,868.78 t.
2. Reactor-inlet molar throughput / the 70-bar anchor throughput.
3. Recycle-compressor MW / the anchor recycle-compressor MW **under the fixed 1.5-bar ΔP assumption**.

The following engineered caps are investigated, each combined with the inlet-composition scenarios:

- uncapped: reference for exact reproducibility of PR #55.
- anchor_1x, anchor_2x, anchor_4x: all three quantities <=1, <=2 or <=4 times their anchor.
- inventory_2x, reactor_inlet_2x, recycle_power_2x: independently constrain one quantity to <=2 times its anchor to attribute any changes.

At each case, the optimizer minimizes the calculated plant cost over **all jointly feasible** (m,p) points. Group ranking, regret, catalyst-vs-temperature reversal kind, Bansode leave-out, matched-group denominator and paper-cluster bootstrap are then recalculated.

**Hard acceptance gate:** unconstrained m<=10 must reproduce all 310 PR #55 candidate optima within numerical tolerance. Every constrained optimum must respect equilibrium, inlet composition and selected equipment caps. Results must count groups lost to infeasibility separately from groups whose rankings agree.

## What cannot be concluded

The fixed \`LOOP_DP_BAR=1.5\` assumption is used by the plant cost model irrespective of candidate loading. Recycle compressor power is therefore an equipment-screening **proxy**, not a mechanical verification of the actual bed pressure drop.

A physically meaningful Ergun or other pressure-drop validation requires for *each* catalyst and reactor configuration: pellet diameter/shape, bed porosity, bed length and cross-section, number of parallel reactors, packed bulk density, gas viscosity and density/composition at temperature and pressure, and the allowable pressure drop. Those items are absent from the printed-comparison cohort. The flow metric is molar throughput, not verified superficial velocity. The fixed-selectivity and one-point conversion surrogate remain additional structural uncertainties. Upstream laboratory feed equilibrium is not a full cooled-bed plant kinetic model; the original five internally cooled reactor points remain a distinct model-domain problem.

**If a cap makes one or more competitors infeasible, a smaller count of ranking reversals is NOT evidence that economic and lab leaders now agree.** Report coverage first.

Run locally from the repository root:

    pip install -r requirements.txt
    python -m unittest discover -s analysis/meoh_engineering_envelope_2026_10_12/tests -v
    for i in 0 1 2 3; do
      python analysis/meoh_engineering_envelope_2026_10_12/run_engineering_envelopes.py shard --index "$i" --count 4 --out "/tmp/meoh_envelope/shard_$i.csv"
    done
    python analysis/meoh_engineering_envelope_2026_10_12/run_engineering_envelopes.py aggregate --input-dir /tmp/meoh_envelope --output /tmp/engineering_reports

This is a controlled process-equipment sensitivity analysis, **not** a validated engineering scale-up design or a new frozen paper result.
