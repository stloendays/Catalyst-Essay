# Evidence: how precious-metal catalyst inventory is financed, and at what cost

Sweep date 2026-10-12. Every quote below was read on the cited page in this session (SEC filings and web pages
in a browser, PDFs as text). Each source has one quote of at most 15 words; numbers taken from tables are
reported as values, not as quotes. Rates that were *derived* by arithmetic, or *applied* to Ru by analogy, are
labelled as such and never appear inside quotation marks.

Three kinds of statement are kept apart:

- **Documented practice** (P): what companies say they do with catalyst metal.
- **Documented rate** (R): a published percentage, with its metal, tenor and date.
- **Model inference** (M): anything we compute or carry over to Ru. These are in `README.md`, not here.

  markets; supplier pages of BASF/Metivo, Heraeus, Johnson Matthey, Umicore; SEC full-text search for

- **Rh lease rates.** No published series found (Rh is in the LPPM sponge standard, S16, but no rate was found).
- **KAAP Ru commercial arrangement.** Searches for KAAP/Kellogg/KBR/BP/Engelhard with ruthenium and lease,

## What the evidence settles, and what it leaves open

- **Settled (P):** leasing, sale-leaseback and consignment of Pt and Pd catalyst metal are established practice in
  refining and chemicals (S01–S06, S09, S10, S14, S15); suppliers also keep customer metal in pool accounts and
  refine it on account (S06–S09, S20). Owning the metal is equally common for nitric-acid gauzes (S23, S24) and
  was the case for Ru held by one KAAP operator (S21).
- **Settled (R):** industrial lessees paid fixed all-in rates of 1.35–5.10 %/y on Pt and Pd catalyst metal in
  2013–2022 (S01–S03, 32 contracts, median 2.10 %). Market-implied Pt lease rates before credit spread were −0.2
  to 0 % before 2020 (S11), 0.8 % on average in 2021 (S12), and spiked to about 7 % (2020), over 10 % (2022) and
  nearly 30 % (2025) (S11–S13). Early-2000s averages: Pt 9 %, Pd 1.45 % (S14).
- **Open:** a Ru rate of any kind; the contract under which KAAP converters hold their Ru; leases longer than 3
  years for any PGM.
