## Extended Data Fig. 6 | Bimetallic surfaces through the ammonia chain, by element layer

Plant cost (no metal recovery, cost optimum inside the 90 m$^3$ bed cap) against log$_{10}$ TOF at 673 K for the Mamun et al. (2019) bimetallic surfaces whose optimum fits the bed cap (global descriptor route). **a**, Transition metals only: 138 surfaces, 5 below the Fe benchmark (15.29 USD t$^{-1}$, dashed), all of them one cheap 3d metal (Fe, Co, Ni, Cu) with Cr, Mo or W (green, 17 surfaces of that family). **b**, Adding surfaces with group 3–5 elements: 222 surfaces, 13 below Fe. **c**, Adding surfaces with sp metals: 406 surfaces, 52 below Fe.
