## Extended Data Fig. 1 | Accuracy of the extraction agent on the methanol literature

**a**, Strict accuracy of the extracted CO$_2$ conversion (X), methanol selectivity (S$_{MeOH}$) and space-time yield (STY) against two curated datasets, by where the value is printed (main-text tables, SI tables, main-text plots, SI plots). **b**, Recall of curated entries per dataset.

## Extended Data Fig. 2 | Full optimizations saved by the lower bound on cost

**a**, Lower bound against full plant cost for the methanol candidates, evaluated in full (blue) or excluded. **b**, The same for the bimetallic ammonia surfaces within the bed limit; dashed, Fe benchmark. **c**, Share of candidates evaluated in full in each search.

## Extended Data Fig. 3 | Robustness of the methanol leader changes

**a**, Paper-cluster bootstrap distribution of the share of comparisons in which the STY leader is not the plant-cost leader. **b**, Disagreements against the regret threshold. **c**, Disagreements under re-measurement of every entry at one, two and four times the measurement error, against the noise floor. **d**, Persistence of each disagreement under re-measurement; triangles, near-infeasible cases. **e**, Disagreements under each robustness variant; hatched, cost gap above 5%. Filled, inert-limited loop (CO, CH$_4$ and N$_2$ at most 6.86% of the reactor inlet); open, no limit.

## Extended Data Fig. 4 | The methanol plant model against published plant assessments

**a**, Cost terms of a reference CO$_2$-to-methanol plant, model against reference. **b**, Model cost against the reported production cost or break-even price of 12 studies at their own inputs; band, ±5%. **c**, Loop metrics relative to the reference plant. **d**, Disagreements in the loop without an inlet limit under plant-model variants.

## Extended Data Fig. 5 | Measured ammonia catalysts of a review through the plant model

**a**, Plant cost against laboratory rate per gram of metal for the primary catalysts of ref. 37, without metal recovery; dashed, Fe benchmark. **b**, Plant cost by metal; bars, lowest cost per metal.

## Extended Data Fig. 6 | Bimetallic surfaces through the ammonia model, by element layer

Plant cost against log$_{10}$ TOF at 673 K for the surfaces whose optimum fits the 90 m$^3$ bed limit; dashed, Fe benchmark. **a**, Transition metals only; green, one of Fe, Co, Ni or Cu with Cr, Mo or W. **b**, With group 3–5 elements. **c**, With sp metals.

## Extended Data Fig. 7 | Supported Ru/C against fused iron with measured rate laws: robustness

**a**, Ru/C − Fe catalyst-dependent cost at Ru financing rates of 0 and 12% per year for the base case and 15 variants, at each catalyst's own optimum (circles) and in the KAAP-type loop (diamonds). **b**, Break-even financing rate of each case; shaded, disclosed catalyst-metal lease rates; red, the reference rate. Each catalyst is optimized inside the pressure range of its rate law.

## Extended Data Fig. 8 | Rank preservation in the Au/TiO$_2$ control and the catalyst-life channel

**a**, Catalyst required relative to 2 nm particles at the nominal size exponents. **b**, Share of draws keeping the full order and the 2 nm leader against the per-size activity scatter σ; dashed, σ = 0.25. **c**, Probability that 2 nm particles stay the least-cost catalyst when catalyst life scales as d$^{λ}$; dashed, λ = n + m. **d**, Full order, 2 nm leader and full inversion against λ with candidate-specific kinetic and operating variation over 273–293 K; shaded, the carbonate-loss regime (λ ≈ 0).

## Extended Data Fig. 9 | Ru against Ni in CO$_2$ methanation, from an independent reactor study

**a**, Ru − Ni catalyst-dependent cost per MWh of SNG against plant scale, central parameters (line) and Monte Carlo median with 5–95% range (points). **b**, Probability that Ru is cheaper. **c**, The two sides of the decision criterion, r$_V$(1 + φ$_{proc}$) and Π, and inventory alone (r$_V$).

## Extended Data Fig. 10 | Purge boundary and inlet-limited methanol loops

**a**, Net production cost along an extended purge grid for every scored entry in the loop without an inlet limit. **b**, Reactor-inlet content of species other than H$_2$ and CO$_2$ along the same grid; shaded, published loop designs; dashed, the loop that anchors the cost model. **c**, Optimal purge and **d**, recycle ratio under each loop treatment. **e**, Disagreements against the inlet limit, counting CO, CH$_4$ and N$_2$ (circles) or CH$_4$ and N$_2$ only (squares).
